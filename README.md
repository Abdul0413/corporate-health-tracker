## Corporate Financial Performance Tracker (Q1 2026):

This repository contains the visual data analysis and source metrics for corporate financial health during the first quarter of 2026. The project focuses on tracking the relationship between revenue, expenses, and overall profitability margins.

## Dashboard Overview: 

The tracker provides a dual-view analysis of the company's fiscal performance:

* **1. Net Profit & Profit Margin %**

A combined line chart and data table highlighting the bottom-line volatility throughout the quarter.

* **January:** Stable start with a net profit of 1,900 and a 15.2% margin.

* **February:** A significant dip into the negative, recording a loss of -3,200 and a margin of -71.11%.

* **March:** A massive recovery phase, peaking at a net profit of 12,700 and a high margin of 89.44%.

## 2. Revenue & Expenses:

A horizontal bar chart comparing gross inflows against operational outflows.

* **Revenue (Green):** Peaked in March, exceeding 14,000.

* **Expenses (Red):** Highest in January (~10,500), with a strategic reduction seen by March.

## ## Financial Formulae Used

The dashboard calculates the health of the organization using standard accounting equations:

$$Net\ Profit = Total\ Revenue - Total\ Expenses$$

$$Profit\ Margin\ \% = \left( \frac{Net\ Profit}{Total\ Revenue} \right) \times 100$$

## ## Analysis & Insights

* **Recovery Velocity:** The transition from February to March indicates a successful pivot or a seasonal revenue surge that effectively neutralized the losses from the previous month.
* **Expense Control:** March shows the highest revenue coupled with the lowest expenses of the quarter, suggesting improved operational efficiency.

* **Margin Volatility:** The wide swing in profit margin (-71% to +89%) suggests a high-leverage business model where small changes in revenue have a massive impact.

## How to Use 

* **Data Updates:** Update the underlying CSV/Excel files with monthly ledger data.

* **Visualization:** The charts are generated to provide an immediate "at-a-glance" status for stakeholders.

* **Reporting:** Use the March data as a benchmark for Q2 operational targets.
